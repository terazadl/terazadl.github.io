About

Researching the systems around AI.

I’m Tera Teng. I write independent, evidence-led essays about artificial intelligence, economics, industrial change, and the institutions that connect them.

我关注 AI 技术如何进入市场、产业与制度，以及这些变化如何重新分配能力、成本与价值。

Current interests

  • Open-weight AI and model economics
  • Technology adoption and industrial structure
  • Money, risk, and economic institutions

Publishing approach

Long-form research is usually English-first or bilingual. Reviews and exploratory notes may remain in Chinese when that makes the argument more precise.
